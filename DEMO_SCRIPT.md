# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard cap 3:00)

Record Tue 3 Nov evening, Wed 4 Nov morning at the latest. Phone: screen recording on (Android quick settings). Laptop: OBS or Xbox Game Bar (Win+Alt+R).
No real children anywhere. Use a doll about 50-70 cm tall, or a soft toy that can stand; an adult for the second take.

| Time | Shot | Voice-over (you) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | You on camera, plain background. | "I'm [your name]. Every month, India's 1.4 million Anganwadi workers are supposed to measure every child under five. Height is the measurement that catches stunting, and it's the one most often skipped or wrong." |
| 0:15-0:30 | Close-up: a tape measure, a doll against a wall. | "Poshan Lens measures a child's height with an ordinary phone, no extra hardware, and tells the worker and the parent what it means." |
| 0:30-1:05 | Phone screen recording: fill the form (name "Asha (demo doll)", girl, 30 months, weight 9.8 kg, Hindi), tap Start, slide the phone left and right. Picture-in-picture: your hand moving the phone. | "The worker enters the age and weight, points the phone and slides it left and right for nine seconds. ARCore tracks exactly how the phone moved." |
| 1:05-1:35 | Result page: orange SAM 3.1 outline on the doll, height in big numbers, "3 measurements agree". Then cut to the tape measure on the doll showing the true height. | "Meta's SAM 3.1 finds the child in every frame from just the word 'child'. My method, Pluto, turns that small camera move into a real height: three independent measurements from different parts of the sweep, and at least two must agree. Tape says __ cm; the phone says __ cm." |
| 1:35-2:05 | Scroll: WHO badges (red "Severe stunting / Refer today"), Hindi parent message, tap "Read aloud" (let it play 5 s). | "The WHO z-scores are computed in code, never by the language model. Muse Spark then explains them: a checklist for the worker, and a message for the mother in Hindi, read aloud for parents who can't read." |
| 2:05-2:20 | Switch language to Telugu, show the same result card printed or as PDF. | "Any Indian language, and a record ready for the growth register." |
| 2:20-2:35 | Quick shot: Muse Spark photo check failing on a bad capture (doll sitting instead of standing), "Photo check: not standing straight". | "If the photo is bad, Muse Spark looks at the frame and says why, before a wrong number reaches a register." |
| 2:35-2:45 | You on camera, or the title card. | "Ten seconds, any Android phone, about fifteen US cents of API per child. Poshan Lens: a screening aid, so no child is missed." |

Fill the blanks with the real numbers from the take you keep. If the measurement is off by more than 3 cm,
show it anyway and say "within __ cm". Judges trust real numbers.

## Shot list to film
1. You, 2 takes of the intro and the outro.
2. A doll against a wall, tape measure beside it (close-up for the cut).
3. Phone screen recording of the whole capture flow, 3 takes. Keep the best.
4. Your hand sliding the phone (second phone or a friend filming).
5. A deliberately bad capture (doll sitting) for the photo-check shot. (Head or feet cut off is caught earlier: those frames are dropped.)
